🛒 How to Buy Bitcoin

Buying Bitcoin is simple if you follow these steps. This guide is for educational purposes only.

1️⃣ Choose an Exchange

First, you need a crypto exchange like Binance, Coinbase or Kraken.

2️⃣ Create an Account

Sign up and verify your identity (KYC) to start trading safely.

3️⃣ Add Payment Method

Connect your bank card or use a bank transfer to deposit money.

4️⃣ Buy Bitcoin

Search for BTC and enter the amount you want to buy.

5️⃣ Store it Safely

Move your crypto to a secure wallet for better protection.
